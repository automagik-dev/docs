## What it does

work executes a plan that review approved. It reads `.genie/wishes/<slug>/WISH.md`, which must carry the status `APPROVED`, or `IN_PROGRESS` when you resume, and sets it to `IN_PROGRESS` before anything starts. The plan stays the source of every instruction.

- **Order.** It works out the waves from the plan's Execution Strategy and each group's `depends-on`. Dependencies come from the plan: a card that shows as ready on the board can still be waiting on another group.
- **Workers.** Each group goes to its own worker through your agent's subagents, with its goal, deliverables, criteria, validation, owned files and stop conditions. Workers that write at the same time get separate files or their own worktrees. A worker in a shared checkout never commits or switches branches; only the coordinator does.
- **Slices.** Inside a group, the thinnest slice that runs end to end goes first, then it widens.
- **Review.** When a worker hands back, a different reviewer checks the group with [review](/genie/skills/review). A FIX-FIRST goes to [fix](/genie/skills/fix) with the group's repair budget. A separate quality pass looks at security, maintainability and performance.
- **Done.** Only after SHIP and passing validation does the coordinator post the verdict on the group's card and mark it done.

The wish stays `IN_PROGRESS` through the pull request and CI. Only a merge plus the plan's QA evidence makes it `SHIPPED`.

## The card conversation

Every group has a card on the board, and the card's timeline is the record of who did what. work posts to it at these moments and no others:

| Moment | Who | What |
|--------|-----|------|
| Dispatch | Coordinator | `genie task comment` naming the worker and the scope |
| Claim | Worker | `genie task checkout` before it changes anything |
| Handoff | Worker | One `genie task report`: the outcome, what changed and where, the validation result, and one `Ruling:` line per decision it took on your behalf |
| Review verdict | Coordinator | `genie task comment` with SHIP, FIX-FIRST or BLOCKED; the evidence goes to `WISH.md` |
| Group done | Coordinator | `genie task done` |

A worker stops to ask only for an irreversible or destructive action, a security decision, a side effect outside its worktree, or a plan too broken to follow. Every other decision is a ruling it takes and records in its report. `genie board --wish <slug>` shows where the groups stand.

## When to use it

- A `WISH.md` that an independent review approved.
- A wish already `IN_PROGRESS` that stopped and needs to resume.

One decided task needs no plan: give it to [wish](/genie/skills/wish). An idea without a plan starts at [brainstorm](/genie/skills/brainstorm), or at wish's plan entry when the decisions are already made.

## Run it

| Agent | How it runs |
|-------|-------------|
| Claude Code | `/work <slug>` |
| Codex | `$work <slug>` |
| Other agents | Its name, or a plain request such as "work this wish" |

work has no saved workflow: it runs in your agent's session and hands the groups to that agent's subagents. Without subagents it says so, and does not claim that an independent review took place. Without the `genie` CLI or its database it tracks the groups in `WISH.md`.
