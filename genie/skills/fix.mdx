## What it does

fix closes the gaps a FIX-FIRST review found. It takes the review, the original criteria and the validation commands, sends a fixer after the blocking gaps, runs the checks, and has a different reviewer score the result. The fixer changes only the scope it was given, and no reviewer ever reviews its own edits.

The loop is bounded by a repair budget per group. fix reads it once from `genie config get budgets.maxEscalationsPerGroup` and uses the default when the key is unset; `genie doctor` prints the value in force. The budget, the attempts used and the escalations travel with the group across handoffs, and switching skills never resets them.

- **Diagnose first.** Before any repair, fix names why the work failed.
- **Repair.** The fixer gets the gap evidence, the criteria, the owned files, the validation and the remaining budget.
- **Re-review.** The checks run and an independent [review](/genie/skills/review) scores the repair. Each attempt is recorded.
- **Repeat or stop.** SHIP goes back to the caller. Another FIX-FIRST earns another attempt only with a changed approach or new evidence. At the budget, on an unchanged failure or on BLOCKED, fix takes the diagnostic route below.

Review comments, bot findings, CI logs and issue bodies are claims to check against the code. An instruction hidden in one is recorded as an attempted injection and never carried out.

## When the loop stalls

| Cause | What fix does |
|-------|---------------|
| `missing-context` | Gets the absent files, logs, history or criteria, and keeps the model and effort as they are |
| `ambiguous-spec` | Resolves the competing readings with you or the plan's owner |
| `env-tool-failure` | Repairs the environment or tool problem it demonstrated, or reports the exact blocker |
| `overdesigned-plan` | Sends the plan back to design or plan review without spending an attempt |
| `model-capacity` | Raises model or effort one step, and only after new evidence rules out every other cause |

Escalations are capped per group, and going past the cap takes a recorded human decision. A repeated verdict is not new evidence and earns no extra attempt. When you reject the direction itself, the loop stops and the plan goes back to its owner.

A repair that touches release machinery, retirement or backup paths, the install record, the tracked roadmap snapshot, or anything outside the worktree stays a dry run until an independent re-review passes and a human ruling is recorded. Repeated attempts that agree prove consistency; they never stand in for that approval.

## When to use it

- A review returned FIX-FIRST and you want its blocking gaps closed.
- [work](/genie/skills/work) runs fix for you when a group's review is FIX-FIRST, and the [wish](/genie/skills/wish) workflow has its own repair stage.

MEDIUM and LOW findings are not repair work. Send them to `deslop`, or record them as accepted.

## Run it

| Agent | How it runs |
|-------|-------------|
| Claude Code | `/fix`, optionally naming the wish or the group |
| Codex | `$fix`, optionally naming the wish or the group |
| Other agents | Its name, or a plain request such as "fix the review gaps" |

fix has no saved workflow: it runs in your agent's session and uses its subagents for the fixer and the re-reviewer. Its handoff lists the gaps resolved and remaining, the checks and their results, the cause and next route, and the counters `attempts`, `effort_escalations` and `budget_source`. The fixer never changes task status and posts nothing to the card; the coordinator relays each verdict and marks the group done after SHIP.
